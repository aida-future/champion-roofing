Process
What happens next

After you hit send

Four steps and no mystery about who does what.

  1. 01

    The office calls you back

    To confirm the details and set a time that works. During office hours that is usually the same day.

  2. 02

    An inspector comes out

    The drone flies three laps around the roof. Ten to fifteen minutes on site, and they talk you through what they found.

  3. 03

    You get it in writing

    A report by email covering what was found, whether or not there is anything to do about it.

  4. 04

    You decide

    No obligation, no follow up pressure. If your roof is fine we will tell you it is fine and you owe us nothing.
