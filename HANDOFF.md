## Why /commercial and /gutters stay on the homepage

Worth recording, since it came up as a question rather than a complaint.

Both stay, for SEO reasons rather than design ones:

- The homepage carries 208 of the 225 ranking rows and nearly all the backlink equity.
  Homepage links are the strongest internal signal available, and both targets need it.
- `/gutters` currently has **no ranking URL at all** against roughly 340 monthly searches
  across the gutter cluster, and gutters is the single most mentioned theme in the Google
  reviews after roofing itself: 13 reviews.
- `/commercial` likewise has no exact ranking URL against roughly 690 monthly searches
  across the commercial cluster.

They are the two clearest ranking opportunities in the whole keyword set, and both are
currently unranked. Removing their homepage links would remove the strongest push available.
